Ribbon icon redesign

The new ribbon icon system (design/icons/SPEC.md) shown in the faithful Carbon Pro Neutral app mock, in every ribbon, overflow menu and flyout, and in an old | new atlas. Every icon goes through a port of icon_theme.dart's _map. The default is the app default: left rail, names off, one column (28 pt glyphs in 36 pt cells). Keys not redrawn yet keep their current drawing and carry a ● mark.
